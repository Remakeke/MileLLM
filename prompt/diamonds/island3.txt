----------------------------------------------------------------------

with features related to financial status and loan characteristics.

The learning objective is to support classification by constructing derived features
that capture financial burden and repayment pressure.

Focus on how credit amount, duration, and installment commitment
jointly determine the structure of financial obligations.

In particular, consider how normalized or interaction-based features
reflect the intensity and distribution of financial burden.
